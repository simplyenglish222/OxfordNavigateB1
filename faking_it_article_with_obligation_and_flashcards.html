Obligation and necessity

Choose the best expression in each sentence.

Grammar note
Present obligation have to need to must
= it is necessary to do something
No necessity don’t have to don’t need to
= it is not necessary
Prohibition can’t mustn’t
= it is not allowed / it is important not to do it
Past obligation had to needed to
We usually use these forms instead of must in the past.
Answers
  1. have to
  2. don’t have to
  3. can’t
  4. don’t need to
  5. mustn’t
  6. must
  7. need to
  8. must
